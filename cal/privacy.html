Calvin Privacy Policy

Calvin has no accounts and does not sell, share, or advertise against your data. Your food log lives on your phone. Some features send specific data to Calvin's server, and this policy sets out exactly which.

What stays on your phone

Everything you log is stored on your device: food entries, calories, macros, weight, goals, recipes, groceries, and your history. Calvin has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no cloud copy of your log. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can export it as CSV at any time from Settings.

Calvin's server

Calvin uses a server (a Cloudflare Worker) for four things: AI photo scanning, food and barcode lookups, verifying a Calvin Plus subscription, and the optional Google Health connection. Everything below describes what is sent and what is kept.

AI photo scanning (Calvin Plus)

When you scan a meal photo with Calvin Plus, the photo is sent to Calvin's server and passed to Google's Gemini API, which returns the foods it recognizes. Google processes the image under its API terms.

The photo itself is not stored. Calvin keeps a one-way hash of it alongside the resulting food list for 24 hours, so retrying the same photo does not cost another scan. The hash cannot be turned back into your picture.

To make results match what you actually eat, Calvin also sends the names and per-serving nutrition of up to 30 of your most recently used saved foods with the request, so a recognized item can be matched to your own product and your own numbers. No other part of your log is sent, and this is only sent on Plus photo scans.

Receipt scanning

Receipt photos are read entirely on your device. The image never leaves your phone. Only the cleaned item text, for example "greek yogurt", may be sent to look up nutrition. Store names, addresses, card numbers, and other receipt details are not sent.

Food and barcode lookups

Searching for a food or scanning a barcode sends the search text or the barcode number to Calvin's server, which looks it up in a public USDA product database, and to Open Food Facts if nothing is found. These requests carry only the search text or barcode.

Subscription and abuse limits

To confirm a Calvin Plus subscription and stop the AI scan endpoint being abused, Calvin sends Apple's App Attest device identifier and your App Store transaction identifier with each scan. Calvin stores daily scan counts against these identifiers. They identify a device and a subscription, not you by name.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Calvin reads activity, energy, and weight data to show trends and import weigh-ins. Apple Health data is read on your device, stays on your device, and is not sent to Calvin's server or anywhere else.

Google Health Connect and Fitbit

This connection is optional and off unless you turn it on. It works differently from Apple Health, because the data comes from Google's servers rather than your phone.

If you connect it, Calvin's server stores:

DataKept
Google access and refresh tokensUntil you disconnect
Daily steps, active calories, distance, and weightUntil deletion is requested (see below)

This data is stored against your device identifier, not your name or email.

Disconnecting in Settings deletes the stored Google tokens, which stops any further syncing. The daily activity and weight rows already synced are not automatically removed. To have them deleted, email the address below and they will be erased.

Purchases

Calvin Plus subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Calvin never sees your payment details.

Analytics and advertising

Calvin has no analytics service, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no data brokers. Calvin records daily totals of AI usage and cost to monitor spending. Those totals are aggregate numbers and are not tied to individual users.

Diagnostics

Calvin uses Apple's on-device MetricKit for crash and performance diagnostics. These reports are handled by Apple's standard, opt-in mechanisms. Calvin operates no collection service of its own.

Your rights

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. For anything held on Calvin's server, which is only ever the Google Health data and the scan counters described above, email the address below and it will be deleted.
