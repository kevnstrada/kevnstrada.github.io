Calvin — Privacy Policy

Calvin is built so that your data never leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Calvin has no accounts, no analytics services, no ads, and no backend. Everything you log — food, calories, weight, goals — is stored on your device only.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Calvin reads activity, energy, and weight data to show trends and import weigh-ins. Health data is read on your device, stays on your device, and is never transmitted anywhere.

Network use

The only network requests Calvin ever makes are food-database searches and barcode lookups against Open Food Facts, available with a Calvin Plus subscription. Those requests contain only the search text or barcode — never your identity, log, or health data.

Purchases

Calvin Plus subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Calvin never sees your payment details.

Diagnostics

Calvin uses Apple's on-device MetricKit for crash and performance diagnostics. These reports are handled by Apple's standard, opt-in mechanisms; Calvin operates no collection service of its own.

Your data, your device

Deleting the app deletes your data. You can export your log as CSV at any time from Settings.
