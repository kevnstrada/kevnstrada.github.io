Free, and staying free

  • Barcode scanning

    Point at a packaged food and log it. No paywall, no scan limit.

  • Food database search

    USDA Branded, with Open Food Facts as a fallback.

  • Full macros

    Protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar and sodium on every entry.

  • Log in under five seconds

    Recents, usuals and common foods are one tap. Copy yesterday's whole log when today is empty.

  • Weight trend

    A smoothed trend line instead of the daily noise the scale gives you.

  • Adaptive calorie target

    Maintenance calories are back-calculated from your real weight trend, so imperfect logging self-corrects against the scale.

  • Apple Health sync

    Reads activity and energy, and imports weigh-ins automatically.

  • Widgets, Siri, CSV export

    Home and Lock Screen widgets, Shortcuts support, and your data out whenever you want it.

No account, no ads

There is no sign-up and no login. Your food log lives on your phone, not on a server — and Calvin does not sell, share, or advertise against it.

Some features do call a server: AI photo scanning, food and barcode lookups, and the optional Fitbit, Whoop, Hevy and Google Health connections. The privacy policy sets out exactly what each one sends and what is kept.
